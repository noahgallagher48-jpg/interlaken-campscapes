What a photograph is depends on where it goes

A photograph becomes what its context makes it. In the daily feed it is a moment your families scan past twice. On the gala screen it is the room going quiet. On a wall it is the camp saying this is who we are.

The same frame can be any of those, and each asks for different treatment. So the library is not handed over as one flat folder. It is organized by context, with each image assigned the job it is built for, the reason it earned that label, and the production it needs to hold up. The value is named at delivery, with reasons and instructions attached, so nothing strong gets spent in a low-stakes post and nothing quiet gets asked to carry a wall. This is stewardship of what the camp now owns.

How it is organized

The labels

Every delivered image carries one of these. The label tells you the job it is built for.

Fine Art

Wall-worthy. Single frames, available light, the place as the subject. Published anywhere at web size with its notation; produced as a print only through the specs below.

Signature Campscape

The hero frames of the place itself: the bridge, the waterfront, the icons of Interlaken. Built to anchor the appeal, the viewbook, the gala.

Development / Campaign

Built for the ask: room for a headline, a face that carries the mission, the frames a donor conversation leans on.

Publication-Ready

Edited and cleared for immediate use in print or digital. Drop it in and go.

Daily / Social

The warmth of the program, ready for the feed. Volume is the job; no notation burden.

Archive

Every usable frame from the residency, processed and ready. The deep well the camp draws from all year.

The print-grade frames

Producing the wall pieces

Why the Fine Art and Signature frames carry production notes, and why it is for the camp.

Produced through Abba Photo at Miller's

The print-grade frames are produced through me at Miller's Imaging Labs. Not a lock-in, a safeguard: the label dies at the wrong printer, and an office print turns a real object into a poster. This keeps the camp holding the real thing.

Each carries its own specs

Paper class, the size window at which the frame reads as intended, finish and mounting where it matters. Delivered alongside the full-resolution file so the frame arrives at full strength.

The notation builds value

Fine-art frames publish with the line "from the fine-art series, available as a print." Publishing an image with its name builds the asset instead of spending it. A camp that knows a frame is a real object gifts it, insures it, and stewards it like one.
