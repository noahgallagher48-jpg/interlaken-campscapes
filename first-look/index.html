The styles and approaches I worked in, and how a camper might experience the space: paths leading to different spaces, and to the same spaces.

Some of these are not what you expected me to be capturing. That is intentional. So is the black and white: where color offered nothing, where black and white stood in for green and brown, or where an image was so classic it deserved a classic treatment.
