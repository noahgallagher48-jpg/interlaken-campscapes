The auction canvas

Camp Interlaken · Summer 2026

Every canvas below is drawn at true size against a nine-foot wall. The sofa is seven feet and the door is six foot eight, so the size reads the way it will read in the room where it hangs.

The auction print, as it stands in the offer sheet: camp buys one canvas at the print price, I make it, and everything it clears at the event goes to Interlaken. Canvas is the right material for these frames; the weave takes the night light without the glare a glazed print would throw across a room. Pick the frame and the size and I will make it.
